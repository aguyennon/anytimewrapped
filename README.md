AnytimeWrapped

Serves as the given name says, check your Spotify Wrapped at anytime throughout the year instead of having to wait to receive your data once a year.

AnytimeWrapped currently features 3 sections -> Create playlist | Delete from playlist | View your data

Create playlist -> 
  - Choose any specific start date and end date as well as a number of songs.
  - AnytimeWrapped grabs that request and dishes back a preview of your top # of songs from that exact period.
  - You then have the option to create a playlist out of those songs that will automatically be created within your spotify and for you to use at any time.

Delete from playlist ->
  - Features a playlist rejuvenation
  - Paste a playlist link or a playlist ID, as well as how many songs you would like to see get pulled from your request.
  - You will also be able to set a 'Skip songs added in the last days' <- You set the amount of days (default 30)
  - AnytimeWrapped will then find your least played songs from the entered playlist.
  - You then have the freedom to delete those songs from the playlist as well as choose which songs from the list of least played songs you would like to keep.

View your data ->
  - Choose which period you would like to view your data in, whether that is yearly or monthly (weekly to come soon)
  - See your minutes listened in the current year (as well as any previous year)
  - See your projected minutes listened by the end of the year.
  - See your daily average.
  - See your minutes per month in graph format with export and copy options.
  - See your top artists this year as well as their plays
  - See your top songs this year as well as their plays
  - See your biggest aritsts plays change of current month compared to the previous month whether the number is positive or negative.

SQLite used as database to store data.
Streamlit used 
Spotipy used to use data from Spotify
