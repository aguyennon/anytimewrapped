AnytimeWrapped

Serves as the given name says, check your Spotify Wrapped at anytime throughout the year instead of having to wait to receive your data once a year.
Will be able to check out your listening history data from all previous years dating from creation of your account. 
Alongside that, you will be able to create any new piece of data like creating new playlists based on your top songs from set timeframes and much more!
Most listened to songs in a playlist, time period/least listened to songs in a playlist for a cleanup etc.

SQLite DB Browser used as database to store data.

More to come as the project develops.
